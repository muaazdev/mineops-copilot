# Underground Ventilation and Gas Monitoring

## 1. Purpose

Sets ventilation and gas monitoring requirements for the underground workings at Jabal Noor Underground.

## 2. Scope

All underground headings, stopes and workshops at Jabal Noor.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet with cap lamp
- Self-rescuer
- Personal gas detector
- Hearing protection

## 5. Procedure

1. Every person underground carries a personal four-gas detector (O2, CO, NO2, H2S) that is bump-tested at the start of the shift.
2. Evacuate the heading if oxygen falls below 19.5% or carbon monoxide exceeds 30 ppm.
3. Diesel equipment may operate only where the airflow is at least 0.06 m³/s per kW of engine power.
4. Auxiliary fans must run before entering a development heading; the vent duct must be within 15 m of the face.
5. Re-entry after blasting only after the gas readings at the face are below the limits and the shift boss approves.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Oxygen deficiency and toxic gases — personal detectors and evacuation limits.
- Diesel exhaust — minimum airflow per kW.
- Blast fumes — re-entry only after clearance readings.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
