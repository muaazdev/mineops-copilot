# Haul Truck Tyre Inspection, Inflation and Change

## 1. Purpose

Covers inspection, nitrogen inflation and changing of large off-the-road (OTR) haul truck tyres.

## 2. Scope

All haul truck tyres; tyre changes are performed only by trained tyre fitters.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Face shield
- Heavy-duty gloves
- Steel-capped boots
- High-visibility vest

## 5. Procedure

1. Inspect tyres at every pre-start for cuts, bulges, exposed cords and heat damage.
2. Inflate tyres with dry nitrogen only; never use compressed air on OTR tyres.
3. Check pressures cold; the target cold pressure is 700 kPa unless the tyre data sheet states otherwise.
4. Any tyre that has run below 80% of the target pressure must be fully deflated and inspected before reinflation.
5. Establish a 30 m exclusion zone around a truck with a suspected heat-damaged tyre and wait 24 hours before approach.
6. Use the tyre handler for all wheel removal; never stand in the trajectory of the rim and lock ring.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Tyre explosion from pyrolysis after heat exposure — 30 m exclusion and 24-hour wait.
- Lock ring ejection during inflation — use an inflation cage and remote inflation line.
- Crushing by a falling wheel — use the tyre handler and wheel chocks.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
