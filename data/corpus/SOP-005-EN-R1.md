# Shift Handover

## 1. Purpose

Ensures safety-critical information passes between outgoing and incoming crews at every shift change.

## 2. Scope

All production and maintenance crews on day and night shifts.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Not applicable — handover takes place in the crib room

## 5. Procedure

1. The outgoing supervisor gives a verbal handover to the incoming supervisor in the crib room.
2. Active isolations are communicated verbally and remain recorded on the isolation register.
3. The incoming supervisor briefs the crew at the pre-shift meeting.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Lost information about active isolations — written handover and joint walk-through.
- Fatigue on night shift — self-assessment and supervisor review (see SP-001).
- Rushing at shift change — 20-minute overlap between crews.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
