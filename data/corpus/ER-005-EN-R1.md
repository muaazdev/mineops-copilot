# Pit Flooding and Dewatering Pump Failure

## 1. Purpose

Defines the response when sump levels rise above the high-level alarm or dewatering pumps fail during heavy rain.

## 2. Scope

In-pit sumps at the open-pit sites and the main sump at Jabal Noor Underground.

## 3. Responsibilities

The emergency response coordinator maintains this plan and runs drills at least every six months. The control room coordinates the response and external services. Every person on site knows the alarm signals, the muster points and their role in an emergency.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Life jacket
- Safety helmet
- High-visibility vest

## 5. Procedure

1. On a high-level alarm (3.0 m), start the standby pump and notify the shift supervisor.
2. If the level keeps rising for 30 minutes, withdraw equipment and persons from the lower benches or levels.
3. At Jabal Noor, if the main sump level reaches 4.0 m, evacuate the levels below the sump per ER-002.
4. Mobilise the diesel emergency pump from the store; it can be operating within 2 hours.
5. Do not drive through standing water deeper than half the wheel height.
6. Record water levels every 30 minutes until the level is back below the pump start level.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Drowning and equipment loss — withdrawal and evacuation triggers.
- Electrocution from submerged electrical equipment — isolate submerged circuits.
- Wall instability after flooding — geotechnical inspection before re-entry.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
