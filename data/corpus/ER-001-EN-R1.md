# Fire on Mobile Equipment

## 1. Purpose

Sets the immediate actions for an operator and the emergency response team when a fire occurs on a haul truck or other mobile machine.

## 2. Scope

All mobile equipment fitted with automatic fire suppression systems.

## 3. Responsibilities

The emergency response coordinator maintains this plan and runs drills at least every six months. The control room coordinates the response and external services. Every person on site knows the alarm signals, the muster points and their role in an emergency.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Fire-retardant coveralls for the emergency response team
- Breathing apparatus for the response team

## 5. Procedure

1. Steer the machine away from fuel bays, other equipment and overhead power lines if it is safe to keep moving.
2. Stop the machine, apply the park brake, lower the body and shut down the engine.
3. Actuate the fire suppression system manually from the cab if it has not discharged automatically.
4. Call "Emergency, emergency, emergency" on the radio with the machine number and location.
5. Evacuate the cab and move at least 50 m upwind; do not return to the machine.
6. Do not open the engine compartment; opening it feeds the fire with oxygen.
7. Treat the tyres as heat-damaged after any fire and apply the 30 m exclusion and 24-hour wait in MM-HT-002.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Re-ignition after suppression — keep 50 m away until the emergency team declares it safe.
- Tyre explosion after fire exposure — 30 m exclusion for 24 hours.
- Toxic smoke — move upwind.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
