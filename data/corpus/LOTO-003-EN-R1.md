# Crusher Isolation and Entry into the Crushing Chamber

## 1. Purpose

Sets the isolation and entry requirements for jaw and cone crushers, including clearing blockages and liner inspection inside the crushing chamber.

## 2. Scope

Primary and secondary crushers, their feeders and discharge conveyors.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Dust mask P2 or better
- Fall-arrest harness when above 1.8 m
- Steel-capped boots

## 5. Procedure

1. Isolate the apron feeder, the crusher main drive and the discharge conveyor per LOTO-001.
2. Isolate the hydraulic setting system and lower the hydraulic pressure to zero; verify on the gauge.
3. Never enter the chamber to clear a blockage while the crusher is energised; use the rock breaker from outside.
4. Ventilate the chamber and test the atmosphere for dust before entry; treat entry as a confined space per SP-004.
5. Install the chamber access platform and anchor harness lanyards to the designated points.
6. Remove loose rock from above the work position before starting liner inspection.
7. A standby person remains at the access door with radio contact for the full entry.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Falling rock from the feed opening — remove loose material and use the overhead protection.
- Hydraulic setting cylinders moving — release pressure and verify on the gauge.
- Engulfment in the feed hopper — the apron feeder must be isolated and the hopper emptied.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
