# Jaw Crusher Liner Inspection and Change

## 1. Purpose

Sets the inspection frequency and replacement criteria for jaw crusher fixed and moving jaw liners.

## 2. Scope

All primary jaw crushers.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Dust mask P2
- Heavy-duty gloves
- Fall-arrest harness

## 5. Procedure

1. Isolate the crusher and enter the chamber only per LOTO-003.
2. Measure liner thickness monthly with a tape measure.
3. Replace jaw liners when the remaining thickness at any point is 30% of the original thickness or less.
4. Use the liner lifting tool and the overhead crane.
5. Reset the closed-side setting (CSS) after the change.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Liner falling during removal — use the lifting tool and keep out of the drop zone.
- Wedge bolt failure — re-torque after 8 hours.
- Frame damage from over-worn liners — replace at 40% remaining thickness.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
