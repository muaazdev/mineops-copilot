# Bucket Teeth and Adapter Replacement

## 1. Purpose

Describes the inspection and replacement of ground engaging tools (teeth, adapters, lip shrouds) on excavator buckets.

## 2. Scope

All excavator and loader buckets.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Face shield
- Heavy-duty gloves
- Steel-capped boots
- Hearing protection

## 5. Procedure

1. Lower the bucket onto timber blocks with the teeth horizontal and isolate the machine per LOTO-004.
2. Replace a tooth when it is worn to the wear line or less than 50 mm of tip remains.
3. Inspect adapters for cracks using dye penetrant every 1000 hours; replace any cracked adapter before it fails in the dig face.
4. Use the hammerless locking tool; never strike hardened pins with a steel hammer.
5. Report every lost tooth immediately so it can be recovered before it reaches the crusher.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Flying metal fragments from striking pins — use hammerless locks and a face shield.
- Bucket movement — bucket must rest on blocks with the machine isolated.
- Tramp metal in the crusher from lost teeth — immediate reporting and metal detection on the conveyor.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
