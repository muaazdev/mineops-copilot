# Light Vehicle Interaction with Heavy Equipment

## 1. Purpose

Prevents collisions between light vehicles and heavy mobile equipment, the highest fatal risk in open-pit mining.

## 2. Scope

All light vehicles, buses and service trucks entering operational areas.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- High-visibility vest
- Safety helmet when outside the vehicle

## 5. Procedure

1. Light vehicles entering the pit must have a valid pit permit, a two-way radio on the haul road channel, a flag whip and an amber beacon.
2. Obtain positive radio communication with a haul truck operator before passing within 50 m of the truck.
3. Approach a parked haul truck only from the front-left (operator side) after eye contact and radio confirmation.
4. Proximity detection alarms on haul trucks warn at 50 m and alarm at 20 m; never ignore a proximity alarm.
5. Light vehicles must not enter the loading area at an excavator while a truck is being loaded.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Collision with a haul truck — radio, proximity detection and approach rules.
- Blind spots of haul trucks — approach only from the operator side.
- Loading area interactions — no light vehicles during loading.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
