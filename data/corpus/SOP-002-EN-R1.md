# Haul Road Traffic Management and Right of Way

## 1. Purpose

Sets the rules for traffic on haul roads shared by heavy and light vehicles.

## 2. Scope

All haul roads, ramps and intersections at the open-pit sites.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- High-visibility vest
- Safety helmet when outside the vehicle

## 5. Procedure

1. Heavy equipment always has right of way over light vehicles.
2. Light vehicles must call on the haul road radio channel before entering a haul road and state their position and direction.
3. Light vehicles carry a 4 m flag whip and a flashing amber beacon.
4. Maintain a minimum following distance of 50 m behind a haul truck, and 100 m on ramps.
5. The speed limit on ramps is 30 km/h loaded and 40 km/h empty; on flat haul roads it is 50 km/h.
6. Keep left on haul roads; overtaking a moving haul truck is prohibited.
7. Never park in a haul truck's blind spot; park at the designated light vehicle bays.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Light vehicle crushed by a haul truck — radio call-up, flags, beacons and right-of-way rules.
- Loss of control on ramps — speed limits and berms at half wheel height.
- Dust reducing visibility — water carts and reduced speed.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
