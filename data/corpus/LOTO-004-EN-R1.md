# Release of Stored Hydraulic Energy on Excavators

## 1. Purpose

Describes how residual hydraulic pressure is released before opening any hydraulic circuit on an excavator.

## 2. Scope

Hydraulic excavators and front shovels at all open-pit sites.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Face shield
- Hydraulic-oil resistant gloves
- Steel-capped boots

## 5. Procedure

1. Lower the boom, stick and bucket fully to the ground; never work under a raised attachment.
2. Stop the engine, apply the swing lock and the travel park brake, and isolate the battery.
3. Cycle each joystick through all functions at least five times to release pilot and main pressure.
4. Open the hydraulic tank breather slowly to release tank pressure.
5. Confirm zero pressure at the diagnostic test port with a gauge before loosening any fitting.
6. Allow hydraulic oil to cool below 50 °C before disconnecting hoses.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- High-pressure fluid injection injury — never search for leaks with a hand; use cardboard.
- Hot oil burns — wait for oil to cool below 50 °C.
- Unexpected boom movement — attachments must rest on the ground.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
