# Blast Exclusion Zone and Clearance

## 1. Purpose

Defines the clearance of persons and equipment from the blast exclusion zone before firing at Falcon Ridge North Pit.

## 2. Scope

All blasts at Falcon Ridge North Pit.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- High-visibility vest
- Hearing protection

## 5. Procedure

1. The shot firer declares the blast time at the pre-shift meeting; blasts are fired at 13:00 only.
2. The exclusion zone is 500 m for persons and 300 m for equipment from the blast perimeter.
3. Sentries are posted at every access road with radios and a blast-guard sign.
4. Sweep the exclusion zone and confirm all persons and equipment are clear before the countdown.
5. Sound the siren for two minutes before firing and one long blast for all clear.
6. Re-entry only after the shot firer has inspected the muck pile for misfires and fumes have cleared, at least 15 minutes after firing.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Fly rock — 500 m exclusion for persons.
- Misfires — inspection by the shot firer before re-entry.
- Blast fumes (nitrogen oxides) — 15-minute wait and wind direction check.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
