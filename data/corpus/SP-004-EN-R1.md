# Confined Space Entry

## 1. Purpose

Controls entry into confined spaces such as crusher chambers, bins, tanks, sumps and pipe culverts.

## 2. Scope

Every confined space listed on the site confined space register.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Full-body harness with rescue line
- Personal four-gas detector
- Safety helmet
- Respirator as per the permit

## 5. Procedure

1. A confined space entry permit is issued by an authorised person before every entry.
2. Isolate all energy per LOTO-001.
3. Test the atmosphere with a gas detector before entry.
4. Oxygen must be between 19.5% and 23.5%.
5. A second person should be nearby during the entry.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Toxic or oxygen-deficient atmosphere — continuous monitoring and limits.
- Engulfment — isolation of feeders and process lines.
- Delayed rescue — standby person and rescue plan in place before entry.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
