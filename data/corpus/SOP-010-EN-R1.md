# Pit Dewatering Operations

## 1. Purpose

Describes routine operation of pit dewatering pumps and sump management.

## 2. Scope

In-pit sumps and dewatering pumps at all open-pit sites and the Jabal Noor main sump.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Life jacket within 2 m of a sump edge
- High-visibility vest
- Steel-capped boots

## 5. Procedure

1. Check sump levels each shift; the pump start level is 1.5 m and the high-level alarm is 3.0 m.
2. Always keep one standby pump available for each duty pump.
3. Inspect the suction strainer weekly and clear debris.
4. Wear a life jacket and work in pairs within 2 m of a sump edge.
5. Record pumped volumes from the flowmeter for the water balance report.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Drowning — life jackets and working in pairs near sumps.
- Pit flooding if pumps fail — standby pumps and high-level alarms (see ER-005).
- Electrical faults in wet areas — RCD protection on all pump circuits.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
