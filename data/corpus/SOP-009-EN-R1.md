# Working at Height on Crusher Structures

## 1. Purpose

Defines fall prevention controls when working on crusher platforms, chutes and the feed hopper.

## 2. Scope

All work above 1.8 m on crusher structures.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Full-body harness with double lanyard
- Safety helmet with chin strap
- Safety glasses
- Steel-capped boots

## 5. Procedure

1. Use fixed platforms and handrails where available; harnesses are required where edge protection is missing.
2. Inspect the harness and lanyards before each use; remove any harness that arrested a fall.
3. Anchor to an engineered anchor point rated for 15 kN; never anchor to handrails or pipework.
4. Maintain 100% tie-off when moving between anchor points using the double lanyard.
5. A rescue plan must be in place before work; suspended workers must be rescued within 15 minutes.
6. Barricade the area below and tie tools to prevent dropped objects.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Falls from height — fixed platforms, harness and 100% tie-off.
- Suspension trauma — rescue within 15 minutes.
- Dropped objects — barricades and tool lanyards.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
