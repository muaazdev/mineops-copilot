# Phosphate Dust Control

## 1. Purpose

Controls airborne phosphate dust from mining, hauling, crushing and stockpiling at Wadi Sahm Phosphate.

## 2. Scope

Pit, haul roads, crushing plant and stockpiles at Wadi Sahm.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Dust mask P2 or better
- Safety glasses
- Safety helmet

## 5. Procedure

1. Water carts spray haul roads at least every 2 hours during the day shift and every 3 hours at night.
2. Dust suppression sprays at the crusher dump hopper and transfer points must operate whenever material flows.
3. Stop haulage on a road when visibility falls below 100 m due to dust.
4. Real-time dust monitors at the plant boundary alarm at 3 mg/m³ (respirable) averaged over 15 minutes.
5. Clean up spillage on conveyors daily to prevent re-entrainment of dust.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Respiratory exposure — water suppression, sprays and P2 masks.
- Reduced visibility on haul roads — stop haulage below 100 m visibility.
- Slippery roads from overwatering — spray in patterns, not continuous flooding.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
