# Hot Work Permit

## 1. Purpose

Controls fire risks from welding, cutting, grinding and other hot work outside the designated workshop bays.

## 2. Scope

All hot work outside the designated welding bays.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Welding helmet
- Leather welding gloves
- Flame-retardant clothing
- Safety boots

## 5. Procedure

1. A hot work permit is required for every hot work task outside the welding bays; it is valid for one shift only.
2. Remove combustible materials within 11 m of the work or cover them with fire blankets.
3. A fire watch with a 9 kg dry-powder extinguisher stays at the work area during hot work and for 30 minutes after.
4. Never perform hot work on tanks, drums or fuel lines until they are gas-freed and tested.
5. On mobile equipment, disable the fire suppression system actuator only with the permit and re-arm it immediately after.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Fire from sparks — 11 m clearance and fire watch.
- Explosion of residual fuel vapours — gas-freeing and testing.
- Burns and arc eye — welding PPE.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
