# Conveyor Belt Tracking and Alignment

## 1. Purpose

Describes how to correct belt mistracking, which causes edge damage and spillage at the tail pulley.

## 2. Scope

All troughed belt conveyors.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Hearing protection
- Gloves
- High-visibility vest

## 5. Procedure

1. Observe the belt running empty and loaded from a safe position outside the guards.
2. Identify where mistracking starts; correct from the tail end towards the head end.
3. Isolate the conveyor per LOTO-002 before adjusting any idler or pulley.
4. Knock idlers forward in the direction of belt travel on the side the belt runs towards, no more than 10 mm at a time.
5. Check that the tail pulley is square to the belt within 2 mm across its face.
6. Clean material build-up from pulleys and return idlers; build-up is the most common cause of mistracking.
7. Run the conveyor for 15 minutes after each adjustment before further changes.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Nip points — never adjust idlers on a running conveyor.
- Spillage clean-up near moving belts — isolate before shovelling under the belt.
- Belt edge damage — correct tracking early.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
