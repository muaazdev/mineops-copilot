# Fatigue Management

## 1. Purpose

Controls the risk of fatigue for workers on 12-hour rotating rosters, with emphasis on night shifts.

## 2. Scope

All employees and contractors, in particular haul truck and mobile equipment operators.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Not applicable

## 5. Procedure

1. Shifts are limited to 12 hours, including handover.
2. No worker may work more than 7 consecutive shifts.
3. Operators take breaks at the discretion of the shift supervisor.
4. Operators who feel tired should inform their supervisor.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Microsleep while operating equipment — breaks, roster limits and immediate reporting.
- Cumulative sleep debt on night blocks — maximum 4 consecutive nights.
- Driving home after a night shift — rest area available on site.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
