# High Voltage Switching and Isolation

## 1. Purpose

Controls switching, isolation and earthing of high voltage (above 1000 V) electrical equipment.

## 2. Scope

Substations, trailing cables for electric shovels and drills, and HV pump motors.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Arc flash suit rated for the incident energy
- Insulating gloves class 2
- Face shield
- Insulating mat

## 5. Procedure

1. Only authorised HV switching operators may switch HV equipment; a switching program is approved before work.
2. Switch off and rack out the circuit breaker; lock the breaker in the racked-out position.
3. Prove the circuit dead with an approved HV voltage detector tested before and after use.
4. Apply portable earths to all phases at the point of work.
5. Issue an electrical access permit listing the isolation and earth points.
6. Cancel the permit and remove earths in reverse order before re-energising.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Arc flash during switching — wear the rated arc flash suit and stand to the side of the panel.
- Back-feed from generators or capacitors — earth all phases at the point of work.
- Damaged trailing cables — inspect cable couplers before energising.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
