# Excavator Swing System Maintenance

## 1. Purpose

Defines inspection and maintenance of the swing motor, swing gearbox and slew ring of hydraulic excavators.

## 2. Scope

Hydraulic excavators of 120 t class and above.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Gloves
- Steel-capped boots
- Fall-arrest harness on the upper structure

## 5. Procedure

1. Release stored hydraulic energy per LOTO-004 and apply the swing lock pin.
2. Check the swing gearbox oil level every 250 hours and take an oil sample every 500 hours.
3. Grease the slew ring through all nipples while rotating the upper structure in 90-degree steps.
4. Measure slew ring bearing clearance with a dial gauge every 2000 hours; record the rocking value.
5. Inspect the swing motor case drain flow; a flow above 8 L/min indicates internal wear.
6. Torque the slew ring bolts to the value on the OEM data sheet in a star pattern.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Rotation of the upper structure during greasing — keep clear of the swing radius and use a spotter.
- Falls from the upper structure — use the handrails and harness.
- Hydraulic fluid injection — test ports only with pressure released.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
