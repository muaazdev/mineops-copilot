# Underground Pump Motor Overload Troubleshooting

## 1. Purpose

Gives the diagnostic sequence when an underground dewatering pump motor trips on overload.

## 2. Scope

Submersible and skid-mounted dewatering pumps at Jabal Noor Underground.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet with cap lamp
- Self-rescuer
- Insulating gloves
- Safety glasses

## 5. Procedure

1. Do not reset an overload more than once; a second trip requires an electrician.
2. Check the motor current on all three phases; an imbalance above 10% indicates a supply or winding fault.
3. Measure insulation resistance with a 1000 V insulation tester; below 2 megohm the motor must be removed.
4. Check the pump for blockage by solids at the suction strainer.
5. Confirm the sump water level; running the pump dry or at snore causes overheating and overload trips.
6. Record the cause on the work order and on the pump log.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Electric shock — isolate per LOTO-001 before opening terminal boxes.
- Flooding of the level if pumping stops — start the standby pump before work (see ER-005).
- Working in wet conditions — use insulating gloves and mats.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
