# Drill Rig Rotation Head Hydraulic Checks

## 1. Purpose

Describes checks on the rotation head hydraulic circuit of blast-hole drill rigs.

## 2. Scope

Rotary and down-the-hole blast-hole drill rigs.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Gloves
- Hearing protection
- Dust mask

## 5. Procedure

1. Lower the mast to the travel position or pin it vertical, and isolate per LOTO-004.
2. Check rotation pressure at the test port; normal drilling pressure is 150 to 180 bar.
3. A pressure drop of more than 20 bar at full flow indicates rotation motor wear.
4. Inspect rotation head hoses for chafing at the mast guides every shift.
5. Check the hydraulic oil temperature; stop drilling if it exceeds 80 °C.
6. Replace the return filter every 500 hours or when the bypass indicator shows.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Falling objects from the mast — keep clear of the mast area during checks.
- High-pressure hydraulic leaks — never feel for leaks by hand.
- Dust exposure — use the dust collector and wear a mask.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
