# Pre-Start Inspection of Mobile Equipment

## 1. Purpose

Ensures every haul truck and mobile machine is inspected by its operator before the start of each shift.

## 2. Scope

All operators of haul trucks, excavators, drills, graders and water carts.

## 3. Responsibilities

The site manager is accountable for implementing this document. Supervisors ensure that workers are trained and competent before they perform the tasks described, and they verify compliance during field inspections. Every worker follows this document, stops work when conditions are unsafe, and reports hazards and incidents to their supervisor without delay.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- High-visibility vest
- Safety glasses
- Steel-capped boots

## 5. Procedure

1. Complete the paper pre-start checklist kept in the cab before moving the machine.
2. Walk around the machine and check tyres, lights, mirrors and fluid leaks.
3. Test the horn, reversing alarm and brakes.
4. Report defects to the shift supervisor by radio; the supervisor decides whether the machine may operate.
5. Hand the completed paper checklist to the supervisor at the end of the shift.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Operating with brake or steering defects — critical defect lockout.
- Walking around a machine with the engine running — keep three points of contact and stay clear of the articulation zone.
- Falls when climbing access ladders — face the ladder and maintain three points of contact.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
