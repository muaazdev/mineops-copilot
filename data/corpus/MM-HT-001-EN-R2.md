# Haul Truck 250-Hour Service

## 1. Purpose

Lists the tasks for the scheduled service of rigid-frame haul trucks.

## 2. Scope

All rigid-frame haul trucks in the Falcon Ridge Mining fleet.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Nitrile gloves
- Steel-capped boots
- Hearing protection

## 5. Procedure

1. Park the truck on level ground in the workshop bay, body down, and isolate per LOTO-001.
2. Take an engine oil sample for laboratory analysis at every 250-hour service.
3. Change the engine oil and oil filters every 500 hours when the previous oil analysis is within limits; otherwise change at 250 hours.
4. Replace the primary and secondary fuel filters every 250 hours.
5. Grease all body pivot pins, steering cylinder pins and suspension pins.
6. Check the brake wear indicators and record the remaining brake pad thickness.
7. Inspect the air filter restriction indicator and replace the primary element if in the red zone.
8. Check the tyre pressures cold and record them (see MM-HT-002).

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Hot engine oil — allow the engine to cool for at least 30 minutes.
- Body falling — the body must be down or pinned with the body-up safety pin.
- Slips on oil spills — clean spills immediately and use absorbent.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
