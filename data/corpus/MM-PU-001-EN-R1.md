# Dewatering Pump Mechanical Seal Replacement

## 1. Purpose

Describes replacement of the mechanical seal on centrifugal dewatering pumps.

## 2. Scope

Pit and underground dewatering pumps.

## 3. Responsibilities

The maintenance superintendent owns this manual and keeps it aligned with the manufacturer's recommendations. Maintenance planners schedule the tasks in the maintenance system. Technicians perform the tasks, record measurements on the work order and report any condition outside the stated limits to their supervisor.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

- Safety helmet
- Safety glasses
- Chemical-resistant gloves
- Steel-capped boots

## 5. Procedure

1. Isolate the pump motor per LOTO-001 and close the suction and discharge valves.
2. Drain the pump casing.
3. Remove the coupling guard and the coupling spacer.
4. Fit a single mechanical seal of the type on the pump data sheet.
5. Set the flush water pressure 1 bar above the pump stuffing box pressure.
6. Check shaft alignment after reassembly.
7. Run the pump and check for seal leakage; up to 10 drops per minute is acceptable.

## 6. Hazards and Controls

- Pressurised water — valves closed and locked, casing drained.
- Rotating shaft — coupling guard refitted before start.
- Heavy components — use lifting equipment for the pump casing.

## 7. Records and Training

Completed checklists, permits and work orders are retained in the site document system for at least five years. Workers complete training on this document before first use and refresher training every two years, or sooner when the document is revised.
